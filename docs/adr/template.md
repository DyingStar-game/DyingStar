measurements that drove it. No story of the discussion; git history keeps that.

## Consequences

What follows from it, good and bad, and **what a change must not do** because of it.

## Rejected alternatives

The plausible options that were tried or weighed, and why each was dropped, so nobody brings one back
by accident.
