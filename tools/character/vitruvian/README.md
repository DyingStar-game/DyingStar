# Character bank (Vitruvian Project -> game)

Builds `assets/_universe/characters/human/human_bank.res`, the skin textures and
`human_catalogue.json`: what the game bakes a body from at runtime (HumanBuilder), from the Vitruvian
Project human by Hopefullyidont1 (CC0; its mesh is Antonia by Olaf Delgado-Friedrichs, CC0).

Source: `VitruvianProject142Pack.blend` (not in the repository, 760 MB): 37 436 vertices, all quads, an
A pose, material zones with their own UVs and 4K maps, 251 shape keys (morphology, gender, origins, age,
FACS face units, phonemes, emotions), eyebrow and eyelash strands.

## Regenerate

```bash
B=build/character   # git-ignored
# 1. mesh, shape keys, weights, rig, strands -- and the textures, raw
blender -b VitruvianProject142Pack.blend --python tools/character/vitruvian/export_vitruvian.py -- $PWD/$B/vitruvian_raw.npz
blender -b VitruvianProject142Pack.blend --python tools/character/vitruvian/dump_textures.py -- $PWD/$B/vitruvian_tex
python3 tools/character/vitruvian/convert_textures.py $B/vitruvian_tex .
# 2. the bank, then the outfits re-fitted on it (needs MPFB's data: tools/character/human_bank)
python3 tools/character/vitruvian/build_vitruvian_bank.py $B/vitruvian_raw.npz $B/human_bank.npz .
python3 tools/character/vitruvian/refit_outfits.py $B/human_bank.npz . --import assets/_universe/characters/humanoids/man_ddurieux.glb.import
python3 tools/character/vitruvian/make_slider_labels.py .
# 2b. hair, eyebrows, eyelashes: generated (tools/character/hair_groom), after the outfits
python3 tools/character/hair_groom/make_textures.py .
python3 tools/character/hair_groom/groom.py $B/human_bank.npz $B/vitruvian_raw.npz .
# 2c. real faces' identity directions from FLAME 2023 Open (tools/character/flame/README.md)
# 3. import the textures, then pack
godot --headless --editor --path . --quit
godot --headless --path . res://tools/character/human_bank/pack_human_bank.tscn -- \
    --npz=$PWD/$B/human_bank.npz --textures=$PWD/$B/textures.json
```

A NEW texture is imported by Godot with its defaults (lossless, no mipmaps): set it to VRAM compressed
with mipmaps (`compress/mode=2`, `mipmaps/generate=true`, `detect_3d/compress_to=0`,
`compress/normal_map=1` for `*_normal`), as the existing `.import` files are.

The catalogue (sliders, origins, asset choices) is **append-only**: an appearance's replicated string
(CharacterAppearance) stores positions in it. The tools that write it stop when an entry would move or go
(`human_bank/catalogue_guard.py`; `DS_CATALOGUE_REORDER=1` overrides, then a CharacterMigrations step must follow),
and `test/unit/test_catalogue_order.gd` checks it against `test/data/character/catalogue_order.json`, where what
is appended to the catalogue is appended too.

## What the game does with it

- **Pose.** Vitruvian rests in an A pose; the game skeleton (53 humanoid bones) and its animation library
  rest in a T pose. The mesh stays as modelled: each bone's skin bind carries its turn from the A
  direction to the template's (`HumanBuilder._pose_skeleton`, `bone_aim`). No re-retargeting.
- **Joints** are affine combinations of the 48 mesh vertices around them (exact on the neutral body), so
  they follow every shape; Rigify's deform bones are merged onto the 53.
- **Zones.** One mesh surface per material zone (Face, Torso, Arms, Legs, Iris, Pupil, Sclera_Cornea,
  Mouth, Tearline), normals computed over the whole welded body.
- **Skin.** One set of maps for everyone: a light and a dark colour mixed by the person's skin tone, a
  normal map from the sculpt's height, roughness, and old-age wrinkles faded in with age.
- **Outfits.** MakeHuman's, re-fitted (`refit_outfits.py`): the bones carry each garment from a MakeHuman
  body to the Vitruvian one, the clearance it had over the skin is restored, and it is bound to the new
  body. 6 of 7 kept (the sport top ends above the Vitruvian bust line).

- **Head and face.** The head is a mesh of its own; seen close up it is baked again with 17 blend shapes
  summed from the FACS keys (`HumanBuilder.LIFE_SHAPES`), which `FaceLife` drives.
- **Hair.** Generated by `tools/character/hair_groom/groom.py` (styles are the `STYLES` table: add one at
  the END, the catalogue is append-only). A style is a shell over the scalp plus guide strands grown on it;
  the game makes the hairs around the guides once per style (`HairStrands`: thousands of strips a fraction
  of a millimetre wide, turned to the camera by `human_hair_strands.gdshader`), as one unskinned mesh on
  the Head bone shared by everyone who wears the style, fitted to each head by a transform. Beards,
  eyebrows (the model's 12 strand sets) and eyelashes are hairs too, bound to the skin so they move with
  the face.
- **Origins.** The model's own origin keys barely move a face: `HumanMacros.ORIGIN_FEATURES` adds, per
  origin, the feature sliders that make its faces.

`test/character/human_preview.tscn -- --count=6 --outfit=0` shows bodies; `--face` the faces,
`--face --still --hair=1 --count=4` hair styles from the first on.
